THE STORY BEHIND THE DAY

Our Story

It started one Sunday...

Way back in 2022, Jasper first noticed Leyanne at church, gracefully playing the piano. Captivated by her quiet elegance, he found every reason to get to know her.

As they served together in their church’s youth ministry, friendship blossomed into love. In time, they each embarked on a journey of faith and service, one went to Pangasinan and the other in Hawaii. Though miles apart, they remained connected through faith, purpose, and love.

Earlier this year, just two weeks after Leyanne returned home, Jasper asked her to marry him, and she said yes.

What started as a simple Sunday encounter became the beginning of their forever. And now, you get to be a part of their story.
