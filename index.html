THE STORY BEHIND THE DAY

Our Story

It started one Sunday...

Way back in 2022, Jasper first noticed Leyanne at church, gracefully playing the piano. Captivated by her quiet elegance, he found every reason to get to know her.

As they served together for the youth at their church, friendship blossomed into love. In time, they each served their missions. Though separated by distance, they remained connected through faith, purpose, and love. Two weeks after Leyanne returned home earlier this year, Jasper asked her to marry him—and she said yes.

The rest is history, and now you get to be a part of it.
